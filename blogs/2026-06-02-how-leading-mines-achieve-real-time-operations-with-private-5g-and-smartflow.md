Combining Ericsson Private 5G with Becker/LASEC's Smartflow digital platform enables mining operations to achieve real-time visibility across ventilation, equipment maintenance, worker safety, and fleet management in surface and underground sites. Practical deployments in Latin American gold mines demonstrate improved personnel tracking, predictive maintenance, and emergency response while reducing energy consumption.
